Which App Lets Me Message My State Legislators?

Federal representatives are one thing — but your state legislators shape the laws that affect your daily life most directly. Here's how to reach them.

Why state legislators matter

State Government Affects Your Daily Life More Than You Think

While federal legislation gets most of the news coverage, state legislatures control many of the laws that affect your everyday life most directly: healthcare at the state level, education funding and curriculum, voting rights and election administration, criminal justice policy, abortion access, LGBTQ+ protections, and much more.

State legislators are also typically much more accessible than federal representatives. They have smaller constituencies, often answer their own phones or email, and attend local events in your community. Constituent contact at the state level can have an outsized impact relative to federal contact.

RepReach focus: RepReach's current primary focus is federal representatives — your U.S. House member and two U.S. Senators. For state-level contact, the tools below cover that ground effectively.

Finding your state legislators

The Best Tools for State Legislative Contact

Your state legislature's official website

Every state legislature has an official website with a representative finder tool. Search for "[your state] legislature find my representative" or "[your state] general assembly find my legislator" to locate it. These tools use your address to identify your specific state senate and state house districts and the legislators who represent them.

vote.gov

The official federal voter information website provides links to each state's official government contact and representative lookup tools. It's a reliable starting point for finding both federal and state-level representatives.

OpenStates.org

OpenStates is a nonprofit project that aggregates state legislative data across all 50 states. You can look up your state legislators, see their contact information, and track bills in your state legislature. It covers state senators and state house members for all states.

Federal + state together
