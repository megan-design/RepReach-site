How to Contact Your Representative About IVF Access

Federal legislation could affect IVF access across the country. Here's how to contact your representative — and how RepReach helps you do it in 60 seconds.

The Issue

What's at Stake

In vitro fertilization (IVF) has helped millions of families have children, but its legal status is increasingly uncertain at both the state and federal level. Some proposed federal legislation that would define personhood at fertilization could effectively ban or severely restrict IVF by treating unused embryos as legally protected persons — making standard IVF protocols legally precarious for clinics and patients alike.

Several federal bills have been introduced both to protect IVF explicitly and to restrict it through personhood frameworks. Where your representatives stand directly affects whether IVF remains accessible to families across the country.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How could federal legislation restrict IVF?
Some proposed personhood legislation would grant legal rights to embryos from the moment of fertilization. Standard IVF involves creating multiple embryos, some of which are unused or discarded. Under personhood frameworks, this could expose clinics and patients to legal liability, effectively making IVF unviable.
Is there federal legislation specifically protecting IVF?
Yes. Several bills have been introduced to explicitly protect IVF access at the federal level, though none have passed as of 2026. Contacting your representatives in support of these measures directly influences their likelihood of receiving a vote.
Does calling about IVF make a difference?
Yes — IVF has proven to be one of the most effective constituent contact issues in recent years, in part because it cuts across party lines and generates high call volumes from constituents who don't typically contact Congress.
How do I find my representative's phone number?
Enter your ZIP code in the RepReach app to instantly see your House representative and both U.S. Senators with their direct district office phone numbers.
