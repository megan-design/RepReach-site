How to Contact Your Representative About the Affordable Care Act

The ACA protects pre-existing condition coverage for millions. Here's how to contact your representative to defend it — and how RepReach makes it easy.

📋 Federal Issue 🔥 High Urgency 🏥 Healthcare 119th Congress

The Issue

What's at Stake

The Affordable Care Act (ACA) provides health coverage to over 45 million Americans through Medicaid expansion, marketplace plans, and protections for pre-existing conditions. Ongoing legislative and regulatory pressure in 2026 threatens to roll back key provisions — including premium subsidies that make coverage affordable for working families, and protections that prevent insurers from denying coverage or charging higher rates based on health history.

Without these protections, millions of Americans with chronic conditions, disabilities, or prior illnesses could find themselves uninsurable or priced out of coverage entirely.

How to Contact Your Representative — Step by Step

1

Find your representatives

Open RepReach and enter your ZIP code to see your House representative and both U.S. Senators with direct office numbers. ACA legislation moves through both chambers.

2

Call — don't just email

Phone calls carry significantly more weight than emails with congressional offices. District office staff track call volume by issue and report directly to the member.

3

Be specific about the ACA provision you care about

Pre-existing condition protections, premium subsidies, Medicaid expansion, and coverage for young adults are all distinct ACA provisions. If one affects you personally, name it.

4

Follow up with a personalized email

RepReach generates a complete personalized email based on your position and any details you share. Send it the same day as your call for maximum impact.

What to Say

Tips for Your Call or Email

📍
State your city first. Constituent calls are logged by location. Your ZIP code is what turns your call into a counted contact.
💊
Name the condition if you're comfortable. Pre-existing conditions are the most emotionally resonant ACA issue. If you or a family member has one, one sentence about it is powerful.
🎯
Pick one specific ask. 'I urge you to protect pre-existing condition coverage' is more actionable for a staffer than a general defense of the ACA.
⏱️
Under 60 seconds is ideal. Staffers take many calls per day. A clear, brief message is easier to log accurately.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ACA provisions are most at risk in 2026?
The most immediately at risk are the enhanced premium subsidies that were extended through recent legislation — if Congress does not renew them, millions of marketplace enrollees will see their premiums spike. Pre-existing condition protections and Medicaid expansion are also ongoing legislative targets.
Does calling my representative actually protect the ACA?
Yes. Congressional call volume on specific issues is tracked closely, and surges of constituent calls have historically influenced votes on healthcare legislation. The ACA itself was nearly repealed in 2017 in part due to sustained constituent pressure reversing votes.
Who should I contact about the ACA?
All three of your federal representatives — your House member and both U.S. Senators. Enter your ZIP code in RepReach to find all three instantly.
What if the ACA doesn't personally affect me?
You can still call on behalf of people you know, your community, or because you believe the policy is right. Your status as a constituent is what matters to a congressional office.
How do I find my representative's number?
Enter your ZIP code in the RepReach app to instantly see your House representative and both U.S. Senators with their direct district office phone numbers.
