How It Works

From your ZIP code to your rep’s phone — in three steps.

Calling your representative is one of the most effective things a constituent can do. RepReach makes it so simple there’s no excuse not to.

1

Enter your ZIP code

RepReach instantly identifies your U.S. Senators, House representative, and state legislators — no searching, no guessing.

2

Pick the issue you care about

Browse urgent actions by category — healthcare, education, civil rights, democracy, and more. Updated weekly from trusted sources.

3

Make the call with your script

RepReach gives you a personalized, ready-to-read script. Tap to call. Done. Your rep’s office records every contact.

Features

Everything you need to stay civically active.

RepReach brings together every tool a concerned citizen needs — in one clean, thoughtful app.

🗺

Rep Finder

Enter your ZIP code and instantly see all your federal and state representatives with their direct phone numbers, party affiliation, and current office status.

📝

Personalized Scripts

Pre-written, customizable call and email scripts for every major issue. Sound confident and informed — without spending an hour researching first.

📰

Legislation Feed

A curated, weekly-updated feed of the most urgent active legislation and federal issues — sourced from the ACLU, Indivisible, 5calls.org, and more.

⚖

Know Your Rights

Clear, plain-language guides to your civil rights and protections — what they are, where they stand, and what you can do to protect them.

🕳

Voting Hub

Registration deadlines, polling locations, ballot information, and upcoming elections — everything you need heading into the 2026 midterms.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Set weekly action reminders, urgent legislative alerts, and upcoming vote notifications. Stay engaged on your schedule, not someone else’s.
