Features

Everything you need to engage

RepReach bundles the tools of civic life into one clean, fast, intuitive app built for people with real schedules.

📍

Find Your Reps

Instantly locate your federal and state representatives by ZIP code. See their contact info, office address, and party in one place.

📝

Personalized Scripts

Ready-to-read call and email scripts in about 60 seconds — written with your constituent ID, a clear ask, and a professional close.

⚖️

Know Your Rights

Plain-language guides to your rights as a constituent, helping you engage with confidence and clarity.

📰

Legislation Feed

A curated feed of current bills, local issues, and public comments — so you're never out of the loop on what's moving through government.

🤝

Get Involved

Discover grassroots resources, petitions, and direct ways to take action beyond just calling — for when you want to do more.

🗳️

Voting Hub

State-by-state voter registration, polling place lookup, absentee rules, early voting info, and ballot research — all in one tab.

Stay on track

Never miss a moment to act

Set push notification reminders so civic action fits into your schedule — not the other way around. RepReach respects your time and works around your life.

Easy onboarding gets you set up in minutes. Then RepReach runs quietly in the background, nudging you only when there's something worth your attention.

  • Set reminders for specific issue categories you care about
  • Choose your frequency — daily digest, weekly summary, or urgent alerts only
  • Never lose your place — pick up right where you left off
  • Track your engagement over time and see your impact
