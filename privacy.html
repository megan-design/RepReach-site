Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: November 9, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the RepReach mobile app (the "App") and the repreach.app website (the "Website") collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with. RepReach is operated by RealPeople, PLLC, a Washington company ("we," "us," "our").

The short version

  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.
  • The App collects basic, anonymous usage data, such as which screens are opened, through Rork and PostHog. It never includes anything you type.
  • There are no RepReach accounts or passwords. Your profile lives on your phone.
  • To work, the App sends some of your information to the services listed below, such as your address to find your district, and your first name, location, and anything you write about yourself to an AI service to write your scripts and drafts.
  • We don't store your personal information on our own servers.

1. Information you provide in the App

  • First name, to personalize the App and your scripts.
  • ZIP code, and optionally your street address, city, and state, to find your federal and state representatives and voting information. A full address is used when a ZIP code isn't precise enough to identify your district.
  • Email address, only if you choose to enter it in your profile. It's stored on your device, and we don't currently use it for anything.
  • What you write, such as your personal story or context for a script, email, or public comment.
  • Notification preferences, such as whether reminders are on and when.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your profile (name, ZIP code, address, city, state, and email if entered), notification settings and history, saved scripts and comment drafts, a log of the actions you've taken in the App (such as calls and emails, with dates), tracked bills, feed topic preferences, and cached representative and legislation data are stored on your device. We don't receive or store copies of them.

3. What we don't collect

  • No accounts or passwords.
  • No device location. The App never asks for location permission or uses GPS. Location comes only from the ZIP code or address you type.
  • No advertising or cross-app tracking. The App doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does collect basic, anonymous usage data, described in section 5.
  • No push notification tokens. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

4. How we use information

Only to run the App: to find your representatives and voting information, to personalize scripts, drafts, and reminders, to show and track the legislation you choose to follow, to manage your subscription, to understand how the App is used through anonymous usage data (see section 5), and, on the Website, to send updates to people who joined our waitlist. We don't use your information for advertising or build profiles about you.

5. Services that receive your information

The App relies on these third-party services. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Rork (app infrastructure and AI service)

  • Many of the App's data requests pass through Rork's hosted servers, including requests containing your ZIP code, address, or approximate coordinates when finding your representatives.
  • Rork's AI service writes the App's AI-generated content. The App sends requests to it directly. See section 6 for exactly what is sent.

Address and district lookup

  • Geocodio: your address or ZIP code, to match you to your legislative districts.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): your address or ZIP code, as a backup when Geocodio is unavailable.
  • U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder: your address, as a backup for district lookup.
  • OpenStates: approximate coordinates or your state, to find your state legislators.
  • Supabase (our database provider): your ZIP code when looking up representative data. Supabase stores only public civic information, such as issues and summaries of public bills and regulations, and no personal information about you.

Voting information

  • Google Civic Information API: your address or ZIP code, to show election and ballot information.

Public government data (no personal information sent)

  • Congress.gov and Regulations.gov: bill, member, and regulation information.

Payments and subscriptions

  • Apple processes all payments. We never see your payment card information.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions for us. It receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information needed to verify purchases.

Website

  • Brevo stores the email addresses of people who join our waitlist and sends our emails. You can unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email.

Usage analytics

  • Rork and PostHog: the App's builder, Rork, includes PostHog analytics. It collects basic usage information: when the App is installed, opened, or closed; which screens you view, including which public bills or regulations you open; your phone's model, iOS version, App version, language, time zone, and screen size; and a random identifier created on your phone, which resets if you delete the App. It does not collect anything you type, your name, email, or address, your taps, or Apple's advertising identifier. This data is sent to Rork and PostHog and is not linked to your name or identity. We use it to understand how the App is used and to improve it.

6. How RepReach uses AI

When you create a script, email, or comment draft, the App sends the needed information to Rork's AI service, a third-party service, which writes the text. Specifically:

  • Call scripts: your first name, city, ZIP code, and state; your representative's name and role; the issue; and your personal story, if you wrote one.
  • Email drafts: your first name and your full mailing address, including street address, because emails to representatives are signed with your name and address; your representative's name and role; the issue; and your personal story, if you wrote one.
  • White House scripts: your first name, city, state, and ZIP code (never your street address); the topic; and your personal story, if you wrote one.
  • Public comment drafts: the regulation's details, your stance, what you wrote, and your name if it's in your profile. No ZIP code, address, or email.
  • Bill and regulation summaries: public information only, such as bill text, official summaries, and legislative history. No personal information.

If you don't write a personal story, some call scripts use a built-in template and no AI request is made. Don't include information in your story that you wouldn't want shared with an AI service. We may change the AI service we use; if we do, we'll update this policy.

7. Messages you send

  • Calls go through your phone carrier.
  • Emails open in your own email app and are sent from your own account, so your email provider handles them.
  • Public comments are copied for you to paste and submit yourself on Regulations.gov.

We don't see, record, or store your calls or sent emails.

8. Public comments are public

If you submit a comment on Regulations.gov or a similar government site, it generally becomes part of a permanent public record, including your name and anything else you include. Requests to change or remove a submitted comment must go to the agency. Avoid including sensitive personal information in comments.

9. How we share information

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only:

  • With the services listed above, as needed to run the App
  • If required by law or legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to protect the safety of any person, including reporting credible threats
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you first

10. Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your device: your information stays until you delete it. Use Settings › Clear All Data to erase everything the App has stored on your device, or delete the App.
  • With service providers: information sent to the services above is handled under their retention policies.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to manage your subscription and meet legal requirements.
  • Website waitlist: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

To ask about or request deletion of information held by us or on our behalf, email app.repreach@gmail.com.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information or to opt out of certain processing. Because we don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, and because most of your information stays on your device, many of these rights are met by default. To make a request, email app.repreach@gmail.com.

12. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the services we work with, and information on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and encryption. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

13. Children

RepReach isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we'll address it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last Updated" date above, and for significant changes we may also let you know in the App or on our website.
