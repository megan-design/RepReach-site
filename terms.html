Description of Service

RepReach is a civic engagement application that helps users identify their elected representatives based on ZIP code and provides communication templates for contacting those representatives about legislative issues. The App includes features such as representative lookup, personalized call and email scripts, a legislation feed, push notification reminders, a Voting Hub, and a Get Involved section connecting users with civic resources.
