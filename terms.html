2. What RepReach is for, and what it isn't

RepReach exists to help everyday people stay informed and take part in their democracy without it becoming a second job. We bring together who represents you, what's moving in Congress, state legislatures, and federal agencies, and how voting works in your state, and we help you say what you think to the people who represent you.

RepReach is for:

  • Finding your federal and state elected officials and how to reach them

3. What the App does

The App:

  • Identifies your federal and state elected representatives based on the ZIP code or address you enter
  • Shows a feed of federal and state legislation and proposed federal regulations, with plain-English summaries
  • Features current issues with background and suggested actions
  • Creates call scripts and email drafts you can use to contact your representatives or the White House
  • Helps you draft public comments, which you submit yourself on Regulations.gov
  • Shows your state's voting rules, deadlines, and links to official election resources, and can send you reminders if you turn on notifications
  • Lets you track bills and see when their status changes
