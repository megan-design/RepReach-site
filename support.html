Common questions

Enter your ZIP code during onboarding or update it anytime in Settings. RepReach uses it to automatically locate your federal senators, House representative, and state legislators. You'll see all of them on your home screen, organized by federal and state.
We curate and update issues regularly — typically weekly — sourcing from trusted civic action organizations including 5calls.org, the ACLU, Indivisible, MoveOn, and others. When something urgent is happening, we move faster. Your feed will always surface the most timely actions first.
Go to Settings › Notifications and toggle on the reminder types you'd like. You can choose from urgent action alerts, weekly issue digests, and legislative updates. Customize the timing and frequency to fit your schedule so RepReach stays helpful, not overwhelming.
Your $1.99/month subscription gives you full access to all RepReach features: representative lookup, personalized scripts, the legislation feed, Know Your Rights guides, the Get Involved section, Voting Hub, and reminders. The subscription supports ongoing issue curation, script writing, and app development. Cancel anytime — no contracts, no hassle.
Open the App Store app › tap your Apple ID photo › Subscriptions › RepReach › Cancel Subscription. Your access continues through the end of your current billing period. If you run into any trouble, email us at [email protected] and we'll help right away.
First, double-check that your ZIP code is entered correctly in Settings. If representatives are still not loading, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, email us at [email protected] with your ZIP code and we'll look into it for you directly.
RepReach is a civic engagement tool. We show all your representatives regardless of party, and we display party affiliation as informational context only. Our issue curation focuses on active legislation and civic actions — the goal is always to help you participate, not to tell you what to think.
